What This Site Collects

This site does not use cookies or analytics, and I do not run tracking tools. Fonts and images are hosted on this site, so loading a page does not send your information to font or media services.

Contact Form

If you send a message, your email address and message go to Formspree, the service that delivers form messages to my inbox. See Formspree’s privacy policy for how it handles data.

Newsletter

The Grounded Tech Journal is published on Substack. If you subscribe, your email address goes to Substack, which manages the list under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser information when you load a page. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Other Sites

Links to other sites, such as LinkedIn, lead to services with their own privacy practices.
